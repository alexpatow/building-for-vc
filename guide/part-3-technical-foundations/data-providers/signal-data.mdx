A new company registration can surface a founder before there’s a website. A burst of engineering job postings tells you something about a company that’s already operating. Buy coverage for the point at which your team wants to reach out.

## What You're Looking For

**Founder signals:**

- New company formations
- Founders in "stealth mode"
- Founder backgrounds and previous exits
- Team composition changes

**Funding signals:**

- Indicators that a company is raising (even before announced)
- Investor interest signals
- Press and media coverage

**Hiring signals:**

- New job postings (especially leadership roles)
- Hiring velocity (how fast they're growing)
- Engineering team growth
- Location expansion

**Web and product signals:**

- Website traffic growth
- Product launches and updates
- App store rankings
- Social media activity

A spike in job postings is worth a look, but it can mean several things. The company might be opening a new office or reposting roles that have been open for months. Give the deal team the postings and the dates behind the alert so they can decide whether to reach out.

## Early Stage Signal Providers

For pre-seed and seed investors looking for companies before they raise or become widely known. These providers often scrape data from government regulatory filings and match them with people data to round out the profile of the company.

## Later Stage Signal Providers

Typically, these providers will be better for Series A+ investors tracking growth signals and company momentum.

| Provider                               | Notes                                                            |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Specter](https://www.tryspecter.com/) | Broad coverage. Easy to use data dumps from S3.                  |
| [Harmonic](https://harmonic.ai/)       | GraphQL API. Supportive of many events in the VC tech ecosystem. |

Try a search from a recent sourcing meeting in each tool. Look at which relevant companies appeared and how many irrelevant ones a partner would have to sift through.

## Considerations

**Speed vs accuracy:** Signal providers prioritize speed over perfection. The data might have gaps or errors, but you're seeing it before anyone else.
