title: "People Data Providers"

For a founder, you might need previous roles, exits, and education. For a company, you might care more about which teams are growing and who just left. Those questions need different fields and different update cadences.

## What You're Looking For

**Founder and executive profiles:**

- Work history and previous companies
- Education and credentials
- Previous exits and successes
- Board positions and advisors

**Team composition:**

- Employee count over time
- Department breakdown (engineering, sales, etc.)
- Leadership team changes
- Key hires and departures

**Talent signals:**

- Where people are moving from/to
- Hiring from strong companies
- Founder quality indicators

If your use case is finding founders from the same research lab, look for dates as well as names. Two people who worked at the same institution ten years apart give you a different introduction path from two people who worked on the same team at the same time.

## Considerations

**Data privacy:** People data has privacy implications. Understand what data you're getting and how it was collected. Some regions and websites have strict rules about processing personal data.

**Enrichment use cases:** People data is often used to enrich signal data (add founder backgrounds to company records, building a holistic view of a company's leadership team, etc.) rather than as a primary source.
