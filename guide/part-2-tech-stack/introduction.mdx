Before choosing a tool, find out who has to use it. An investor may ask for a portfolio dashboard, but the CFO knows where the valuations come from and the platform team knows which founders will send updates.

## Who to work with

| Workflow                                                          | Start with                                  | What they need                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Research](/guide/part-2-tech-stack/research-platforms)           | Partners and associates                     | Connect research across deals and develop a thesis                  |
| [Sourcing](/guide/part-2-tech-stack/sourcing-tools)               | Deal team                                   | Find companies and follow up while they’re still relevant           |
| [CRM](/guide/part-2-tech-stack/crm-and-deal-flow)                 | Investment team                             | Know who spoke to a founder and what happens next                   |
| [Fund operations](/guide/part-2-tech-stack/fund-operations)       | CFO, fund administrator                     | Reconcile investments, capital calls, distributions, and LP reports |
| [Portfolio support](/guide/part-2-tech-stack/portfolio-support)   | Founders, platform team, operating partners | Help with hiring, customers, and introductions                      |
| [Fundraising](/guide/part-2-tech-stack/fundraising)               | Managing partner, investor relations        | Track LP conversations and produce materials                        |
| [Website](/guide/part-2-tech-stack/website-and-external-presence) | Marketing, investor relations               | Publish the thesis, team, and portfolio                             |

The [VC Stack directory](https://www.vcstack.com/data-software-tools) lists data providers and software for VC funds.

If you’re choosing your first project, [Putting It Together](/guide/part-2-tech-stack/putting-it-together) covers the order I’d tackle these in.
