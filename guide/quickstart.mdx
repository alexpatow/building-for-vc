## Where to start

- **New to VC?** Start with [how a fund works](/guide/part-1-understanding-vc/what-is-a-vc-fund), then [look at your own fund’s portfolio](/guide/part-1-understanding-vc/understanding-your-vc-fund).
- **First technical hire?** [Common Mistakes](/guide/part-1-understanding-vc/common-mistakes) covers the conversations to have early. [Putting It Together](/guide/part-2-tech-stack/putting-it-together) helps you choose the first project, including when you inherit an underused stack.
- **Coming from investing or fund ops?** Pick a workflow in [The VC Tech Stack](/guide/part-2-tech-stack/introduction). If a vendor or engineer starts talking about a warehouse, start with [what it can tell you about last quarter’s pipeline](/guide/part-3-technical-foundations/data-warehousing).

- **Hiring that person?** See [Hiring Your Data Team](/guide/part-1-understanding-vc/hiring-your-data-team).
