I wrote this for the engineer joining a VC fund who knows how to build software and is still figuring out how the partners make investment decisions. I wrote it for the love of the game. It’s the guide I wish I’d had when I started building in VC.

If you’ve spent years in a fund and are now building your own tools, you can come at this from the other direction. Part 2 starts from the work you already know. Part 3 explains what happens underneath it, from matching a company across two datasets to deciding who can see the result.

[Alex Patow](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexpatow/) runs [Boolean Industries](https://boolean.industries), providing engineering services for venture capital and private equity.
